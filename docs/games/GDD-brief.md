# Game Design Brief — ebby-title-01

> One page. If it grows past one page, we are designing, not deciding.

## Pillar sentence

> "A 60-second arcade rush where every click feels worth it."

## Core fantasy

You are sharper, faster, and luckier than the screen wants you to be.

## Loop (30 seconds)

```
START → spot target → click → reward (juice + score) → next target → win/lose
```

- **Start** — game begins immediately, no menu friction.
- **Verb** — single mouse click (or tap on web/mobile).
- **Feedback** — within 80 ms of input: sound, screen shake, color flash.
- **Win** — 10 hits, then encourage one more run.

## Constraints (this is what makes it cheap)

- One screen, no scrolling.
- No accounts, no save game, no menus beyond a retry hint.
- No art budget in week 1 — placeholder shapes only.
- 60 fps on a 5-year-old laptop and any modern phone browser.

## Out of scope (week 1)

- Multiplayer, leaderboards, monetisation, narrative, tutorial, settings.
- Anything that takes more than half a day to prototype.

## Success metrics (Friday playtest)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Time-to-first-input from page load | < 3 s |
| "Would you play again?" yes | ≥ 6 / 10 testers |
| Median play time per session | ≥ 90 s |
| Bugs that block the loop | 0 |

## Open questions (decide by Wednesday)

1. Click-to-pop or click-and-drag? (Pick one — A/B in playtest.)
2. Is there a timer, or only a score target?
3. Does the target move while alive, or only respawn after hit?

Whoever owns the question writes their name and the decision date next to it.
